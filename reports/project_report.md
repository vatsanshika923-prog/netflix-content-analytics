# Netflix Content Analytics Report

## Objective

Analyze Netflix's content catalog to identify trends in content distribution, growth, ratings, genres, and country contributions.

## Dataset

Netflix Titles Dataset

## Data Cleaning

* Removed duplicate records
* Handled missing values
* Converted date_added to datetime format
* Created year_added feature
* Removed 98 records with missing date information

## Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis included:

* Movies vs TV Shows distribution
* Content growth over time
* Top content producing countries
* Content ratings analysis
* Genre analysis
* Director analysis
* Release year trends

## Key Findings

* Movies dominate Netflix's content catalog.
* Netflix expanded rapidly after 2015.
* The United States contributes the largest amount of content.
* TV-MA and TV-14 are the most common ratings.
* Drama and International Movies are among the most represented genres.

## Recommendations

* Expand international content production.
* Invest in high-performing genres.
* Strengthen regional content offerings.
* Maintain content diversity.

## Conclusion

Netflix has developed a large and diverse content catalog with strong international growth. The analysis highlights important trends that can support future content strategy decisions.
